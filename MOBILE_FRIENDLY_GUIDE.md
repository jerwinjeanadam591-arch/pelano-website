The Pelano Resources website has been fully optimized for mobile devices while maintaining desktop display quality. The website now perfectly adapts to all screen sizes from **320px (small phones) to 2560px (large desktops)**.

### 1. **Responsive Navbar & Navigation**

- ✅ Social links properly sized
- ✅ Text sizes adjusted for mobile

- ✅ Text contrast maintained on mobile
