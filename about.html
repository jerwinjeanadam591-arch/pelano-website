About Us

Learn more about Pelano Resources Ltd

Industry Leaders in Forest Products

Pelano Resources Ltd is a high-quality forest products supplier based in Mafinga, Tanzania, specialising in premium treated timber and utility poles for contractors, builders, manufacturers, and enterprises across Tanzania and East Africa. Focused on quality and professional standards, we source, treat, and deliver timber that conforms to recognised industry standards while keeping pricing competitive. Our Mafinga facility provides modern storage and handling, and our experienced team offers expert product selection, quality checks, and reliable customer support for projects of all sizes.

Our Mission

To provide high-quality resources and products that meet the needs of our customers while maintaining the highest standards of integrity, sustainability, and customer service. We are committed to contributing positively to the development of Mafinga and Tanzania as a whole.

Our Vision

To become the leading provider of quality resources in Tanzania, recognized for excellence, reliability, and innovation. We aspire to expand our reach while maintaining our commitment to quality and customer satisfaction.
