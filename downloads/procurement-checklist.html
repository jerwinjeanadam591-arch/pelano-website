Forest products procurement checklist

Pelano Resources Ltd · Mafinga, Tanzania

Use this checklist to organize your purchase enquiry. Confirm commercial and technical details in writing before placing an order.

Enquiry details

  • Company and contact person
  • Product and intended application
  • Specifications, dimensions and treatment requirements
  • Quantity, units and packaging
  • Delivery destination and site access considerations
  • Required delivery date or project timeframe
  • Required certificates, inspection or supporting documents
  • Purchase order and invoicing requirements

Before order confirmation

  • Confirm exact specification and acceptance criteria.
  • Confirm availability, total pricing, taxes, freight and payment terms.
  • Confirm dispatch and delivery dates.
  • Confirm document scope and responsible contacts.
