Timber selection guide

Pelano Resources Ltd · Mafinga, Tanzania

This checklist helps buyers prepare timber requirements. It is general procurement guidance, not a substitute for a design professional, applicable standards or project-specific technical advice.

Before requesting a quote

  1. Describe the intended use and whether the timber is structural, exterior, ground-contact or temporary.
  2. Specify species or grade only when established by your project requirements.
  3. List dimensions, tolerances, quantity and packaging.
  4. State treatment requirements and the applicable specification or standard, if known.
  5. Provide delivery destination and required delivery timeframe.
  6. Ask for current availability, technical documents and treatment evidence relevant to the order.

Important

Product suitability, treatment class, certification, availability, pricing and transport must be confirmed by Pelano Resources for the specific order before purchase.
