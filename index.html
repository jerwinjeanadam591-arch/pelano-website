About Pelano Resources

Pelano Resources Ltd is a leading supplier of premium forest products based in Mafinga, Tanzania. With years of industry expertise, we specialize in treated timber, utility poles, and high-grade plywood for construction, industrial, and commercial applications.

We are committed to providing world-class forest products that meet international quality standards. Our products serve contractors, builders, industries, and businesses across Tanzania and beyond, backed by reliable customer service and professional support.
