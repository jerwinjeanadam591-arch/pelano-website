Industry supply questions

Which products can I enquire about?

Pelano Resources lists treated timber, utility poles, telecom poles, pallets and railway sleepers. Check the product catalogue and ask the team to confirm current options.

What information should a project enquiry include?

Include the product and application, estimated quantity, required dimensions or specification, destination, timeframe and any documentation requirements.

Does a product category confirm that an item is suitable for my project?

No. The responsible project professional should define technical requirements. Confirm the offered product, applicable documents and intended use before ordering.
