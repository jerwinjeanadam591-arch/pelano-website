How we protect your personal information

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 28, 2026

1. Introduction

Pelano Resources Ltd ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect information about you in a variety of ways. The information we may collect on the Site includes:

  • Enquiry details: Contact details you enter into an enquiry form remain in your browser until you choose to open your email or WhatsApp app. This static website does not transmit them to a Pelano Resources server.
  • Browser storage: The site may store preferences (such as language and dark mode) and local enquiry reference metadata (reference code, date, channel, and selected product names) in your browser. These references do not confirm that an enquiry was received.
  • Newsletter: If the newsletter service is enabled, your email address and consent time are stored by Pelano Resources' configured service while your subscription is pending confirmation or active. We add addresses to the mailing list only after you confirm by email. You can unsubscribe using the link in a newsletter or contact Pelano Resources.
  • Third-party services: When you choose to send a prepared message, your email provider or WhatsApp receives information according to its own settings and privacy policy. The site does not control those services.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect in the following ways:

  • To prepare enquiry messages for you to send to Pelano Resources
  • To remember website preferences and allow local lookup of enquiry reference metadata

4. Data Security

This static website does not receive or store the personal details entered into its enquiry forms. Browser-side validation and reference codes do not replace server-side security or confirm message delivery.

  • Keep your browser and device secure.
  • Review the privacy and security practices of the email or messaging provider you choose.
  • Do not include confidential or sensitive information in an enquiry unless it is necessary.

5. Third-Party Services

Opening an email or WhatsApp link transfers information only when you choose to send it, through the provider you select. Embedded maps or other third-party content may also be subject to that provider's privacy policy.

  • Google Maps for the map embedded on the contact page
  • Your selected email or messaging provider when you send an enquiry

6. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right to Access: You can request a copy of your personal data
  • Right to Correction: You can request correction of inaccurate data
  • Right to Deletion: You can request deletion of your personal data
  • Right to Opt-Out: You can unsubscribe from marketing communications

7. Cookies and Tracking

The site uses browser local storage for preferences and enquiry reference metadata. Optional Google Analytics is disabled unless Pelano Resources configures it and you choose to allow it. If enabled, analytics records page and interaction events only; form contents, contact details and search terms are not sent. You can reject or change this choice at any time. Enquiry references remain only in the browser where they were created. Newsletter confirmation links use a temporary confirmation token; unsubscribe links remain available for you to withdraw consent.

8. Children's Privacy

Our website is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that a child has provided us with personal information, we will delete such information immediately.

9. Privacy Requests

For questions about this notice or requests relating to information you shared directly with Pelano Resources, contact us using the details below. Enquiry references stored in your browser can be removed by clearing this site's browser storage. Information sent through email or WhatsApp is handled by the provider you choose.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our privacy practices, please contact us at:

  • Email: info@pelanoresources.co.tz
  • Phone: +255 755 885 888
  • Address: Mafinga, Iringa, Tanzania

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date above.

12. Effective Date

This Privacy Policy is effective as of May 28, 2026.
